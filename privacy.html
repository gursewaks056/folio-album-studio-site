Privacy policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

This policy describes Folio Album Studio, its public support website, and support provided by Gursewak Singh. For privacy questions, email gs.products.work@gmail.com.

Photo processing stays on your computer

Folio Album Studio processes imported photos and album layouts locally. The application does not upload your photos, project files, source file paths or exports to a Folio server. It has no Folio account system, advertising, analytics SDK or application telemetry service.

Files stored on your device

The app creates thumbnails, preview caches and a recovery snapshot in operating-system application-data locations. Saved projects contain photo references, file paths, layout settings and related metadata; they do not embed your original photos. Exports are saved where you choose.

Native photo processing uses temporary disk files, normally removed when an operation ends. A crash or interrupted operation can leave temporary data behind. Cache and recovery files can remain until removed. Uninstall behavior varies between standalone and packaged installations. Back up originals and saved projects before deleting application data. The app does not delete or modify your original photos.

If you choose a folder synchronized by OneDrive or another cloud service, that service may upload files under your own synchronization settings. This is separate from Folio's local processing.

Windows, runtime and Microsoft Store services

The Windows interface uses Microsoft Edge WebView2. Windows, WebView2, Microsoft Store, runtime updates and diagnostics may communicate with Microsoft under their own settings and privacy terms. Local Folio photo processing does not mean that the operating system or runtime never makes network requests.

If you buy through Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles checkout and payment details. The Folio application does not request or store card numbers. Microsoft may make transaction, sales and payout information available to the publisher through its developer services.

See the Microsoft Privacy Statement for Microsoft's services.

Support email

When you email support, the publisher receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond, investigate reported issues and maintain necessary support records. Support is provided through Gmail, so Google processes email under its applicable terms and privacy policy.

Send only the information needed for your request. Do not send passwords, card details, private photos or complete project files unless necessary and agreed. Projects and logs may contain personal file names and paths; review and redact them first. Reproducing an issue with a non-sensitive sample is preferable.

This website

These static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The pages do not add analytics, advertising, cookies, embedded tracking or contact forms. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses and other request information for security and service operation. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Opening an email link uses your chosen email service rather than submitting a form on this site.

Use, disclosure and retention

The publisher does not sell personal information received through support or use it for advertising. Support correspondence and any developer transaction records are kept as needed for support, security, dispute resolution and applicable legal or accounting obligations. Information may be processed by the service providers described above or disclosed when legally required.

Local photo and project data remain under your control. You choose where to save, share or delete them. Review project files before sharing: they can reveal local file names and paths. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own under their respective policies.

Privacy requests

You can contact gs.products.work@gmail.com to ask about personal information held in support correspondence or request access, correction or deletion, subject to applicable law, identity verification and required retention. Rights vary by jurisdiction. The publisher cannot access or delete files stored only on your device; manage those locally. Microsoft purchase/account requests should also be directed to Microsoft where it controls the relevant data.

Policy changes

Changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Review this policy when using a newer app version or contacting support.
