Support

For help with Folio Album Studio, contact Gursewak Singh at gs.products.work@gmail.com.

Please use the subject Folio Album Studio support. Support is by email; response times are not guaranteed.

What to include

  • Your app version, available in the About dialog, and whether it is the Microsoft Store or standalone edition.
  • Your Windows version and a short description of what you expected and what happened.
  • The steps that reproduce the problem and the exact error text, with personal paths redacted.
  • For import problems: file format, camera model if relevant, and image pixel dimensions.
  • For export problems: PDF or TIFF, sheet dimensions, DPI, and available output/temporary disk space.

Do not send passwords, payment details or private photos. Share screenshots or non-sensitive samples only after reviewing them. Project files may contain photo names and paths. See our privacy policy before sending information.

A photo does not import

Confirm the original file exists and is readable. Try copying it to an accessible local folder and importing again. RAW support depends on the camera and bundled decoder; damaged files or unsupported camera variants can fail. File size alone does not guarantee compatibility. If the error mentions disk space or temporary storage, free sufficient space before retrying.

Photos are missing after opening a project

Projects reference originals rather than embedding them. Moving photos or opening a project on another computer can break those references. Copy the originals to the new computer, select an affected photo and use Relink selected photo. Save a separate project copy when moving between computers.

PDF or TIFF export fails

Check that all original photos are available, the destination is writable and there is enough space on both the temporary and output drives. Large sheets and high DPI can require substantial disk space and time. Export uses originals, not just the preview images. Keep original files accessible until export finishes.

Backup and recovery

Save projects regularly and keep separate backups of originals. Recovery is not a substitute for a backup. Uninstalling the app or deleting its application data can remove caches and recovery data. Preserve your work before reinstalling or clearing app data.

Microsoft Store purchases and refunds

Microsoft handles Store checkout, billing and refund eligibility. Use your Microsoft account order history or Microsoft Support for payment and refund requests. Do not email card details to the publisher. Contact the publisher for technical issues with the app.
